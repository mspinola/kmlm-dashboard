How this page reads KMLM

A rules-based trend index, from the fund's own daily holdings files

Source. KraneShares publishes KMLM's full holdings every day and keeps each dated file online; this page has read every one from January 2, 2024. The fund tracks the MLM Index: 22 futures markets (commodities, currencies and government bonds, no equity index futures), each long or short by its price against a long moving average, weighted across markets by volatility.

Futures are counted at notional value, signed: negative is short, as a percentage of net assets (cash, T-bills and small foreign-currency balances). A negative currency line is short that currency against the dollar.

Decisions, not drift. The file gives contracts as well as notional. Between trades the contract count does not change, so a weight that moves while the count stays put is the price moving, and this page does not call it a decision. A change in the count is a trade, after taking out two things that are not: creations and redemptions, which scale every market by the same factor on the same day, and contract rolls, where a new month can carry a slightly different count for the same exposure.

Flips are walked, not jumped. When a market's signal turns, the fund does not cross from long to short in one day. It trades in steps of a similar size, usually several sessions running the same way (from 2024 to late 2026, three in five runs of steps lasted two sessions or more and one in ten lasted twelve or more), so a market is shown as trading while it walks and a flip is marked on the file on which the position crossed zero.

Sizes reset monthly. On the first trading day of each month the index resets its volatility weights, and most markets are resized a little. Those resizes are shown apart from the walks and are not read as decisions.

Notional is not risk. A 10-year bond and a soybean future of the same notional carry very different risk, and the index sizes by volatility, so the bond lines run large.
